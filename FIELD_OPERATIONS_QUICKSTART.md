# ARTPARK 2026 — Field Operations Quickstart

## Mission Control

Open `/admin` on any internet-connected phone or laptop and enter the shared team passphrase. Use **ISSUE NEXT FIELD CODE** instead of opening the private credential inventory. A real credential becomes durably owned on first activation and is never issued to another player by normal operations. Select **LOG OUT** when finished.

`RESET GAMEPLAY` clears the digital route, responses, and final reflection while preserving durable identity, recovery continuity, profile/history, and prize history. It does not affect physical stamps. **DELETE PLAYER IDENTITY + RELEASE CREDENTIAL** is a separate destructive operator action requiring typed code confirmation; it removes active identity/profile/recovery state and permits reuse, while historical prize and audit records remain attached to the credential. `TEST-01` through `TEST-05` remain explicitly reusable fixtures and do not change event counts.

## What you need at concierge

Minimum powerless setup:

- printed player cards;
- printed unique access-code credentials;
- small token prizes;
- normal puzzle/stamp materials.

Recommended but optional:

- your phone;
- charged power bank;
- laptop if convenient.

Your hotspot is **not** used by players. Players use their own cellular data.

## When a player joins

1. Give the player/group one player card.
2. Give them one unused unique field access code.
3. Tell them to keep the code with the group.
4. Do not activate anything manually.
5. They enter the code themselves on their first QR scan.

## If you have no power

Keep handing out printed cards and codes. The cloud system continues running independently.

## If you have no hotspot or concierge internet

Same procedure. Your dashboard is temporarily unavailable, but player phones still talk directly to the cloud over their own data.

## If a player's phone dies

They can enter the same unique code on another phone. Their same durable identity, retained profile, and current route reappear. After a gameplay reset, the same code restores the same identity with fresh quest progress.

## If a player says a station did not register

If you have internet, open `/admin`, look up their code, and inspect the route. If you do not have internet, use the physical stamp/card as your immediate field authority and repair the digital record later if needed.

## What counts as physical completion

The rubber stamp on the card, not the QR scan. The QR network controls narrative/video progression; the lockbox stamp proves the physical puzzle was solved.

## End of route

Verify the physical card according to the event rules, give the small completion prize, and direct them into the hidden-key/final-broadcast phase.
